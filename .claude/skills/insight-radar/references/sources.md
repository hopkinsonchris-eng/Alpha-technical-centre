# Source registry

Where to look, discipline by discipline. Work down the tiers: societies and
journals first, trade press only to corroborate or to catch commercial news the
journals miss.

Access notes matter. Much of the society literature is paywalled, and you will
often only see an abstract. An abstract is a citable source, but say so — mark
it `abstract` and never describe results you could not read. Do not paraphrase
a paywalled paper from a press release about it and cite the paper.

---

## Learned societies and their publications

**SPE — Society of Petroleum Engineers.** The spine of this series.
- `onepetro.org` — the aggregate archive for SPE, OTC, SPWLA, ARMA, ISRM,
  URTeC. Search here first for anything reservoir, production, drilling or
  completions. Mostly paywalled; abstracts are open.
- `jpt.spe.org` — Journal of Petroleum Technology. Technical-magazine tier
  (E2), open, and the fastest read on what the society thinks matters. Good
  for finding the papers worth chasing.
- SPE peer-reviewed journals (E1): *SPE Journal*, *SPE Reservoir Evaluation &
  Engineering*, *SPE Production & Operations*, *SPE Drilling & Completion*,
  *SPE Reservoir Engineering*.
- Conference proceedings (E1 where refereed): ATCE, SPE Reservoir Simulation
  Conference, Hydraulic Fracturing Technology Conference, Digital Energy,
  Artificial Lift, IOR, ADIPEC, EAGE/SPE Europec, LACPEC (Latin America).

**SEG — Society of Exploration Geophysicists.** Seismic acquisition,
processing, imaging, interpretation, machine learning on seismic.
- `library.seg.org` — *Geophysics* (E1), *Interpretation* (E1),
  *The Leading Edge* (E2, the readable one), Annual Meeting expanded abstracts.
- `seg.org/publications` for open items and technical committee output.

**AAPG — American Association of Petroleum Geologists.** Basin analysis,
petroleum systems, stratigraphy, geological modelling, unconventionals.
- `pubs.geoscienceworld.org/aapgbull` — *AAPG Bulletin* (E1).
- `explorer.aapg.org` — *AAPG Explorer* (E2, open).
- `datapages.com` — Search and Discovery, ACE abstracts, regional archives
  (strong Latin America and Gulf Coast coverage).

**SPWLA — Society of Petrophysicists and Well Log Analysts.** The primary
source for petrophysical evaluation.
- *Petrophysics* journal (E1) and Annual Logging Symposium transactions,
  via `spwla.org` and OnePetro.

**EAGE — European Association of Geoscientists and Engineers.**
- `earthdoc.org` — *Geophysical Prospecting* (E1), *Petroleum Geoscience* (E1),
  *First Break* (E2), Annual Conference abstracts.

**Geological societies.**
- Geological Society of London — `lyellcollection.org`: *Petroleum Geoscience*,
  *Journal of the Geological Society*, Special Publications (E1). Strong on
  reservoir characterisation and structural geology.
- GSA — `geosociety.org`, *GSA Bulletin*, *Geology* (E1).
- SEPM — `sepm.org`, *Journal of Sedimentary Research* (E1) for
  facies and depositional models feeding geological modelling.
- Regional societies worth a sweep when the topic fits: HGS (Houston
  Geological Society), GCAGS / Gulf Coast Association of Geological Societies,
  ALAGO and SVG (Latin America geophysics/geology), Petroleum Exploration
  Society of Great Britain.

**Adjacent engineering bodies.**
- ASME (`asme.org`) — rotating equipment, pressure systems, surface facilities.
- NACE / AMPP (`ampp.org`) — corrosion, materials, integrity.
- API (`api.org`) — standards changes; a new or revised API standard is a real
  development and often more consequential than a paper.
- IOGP (`iogp.org`) — safety and operating practice reports, open.
- ASCE, IEEE (electrification, subsea power, control systems).

## Journals outside the societies

- Elsevier: *Journal of Petroleum Science and Engineering* (now *Geoenergy
  Science and Engineering*), *Fuel*, *Applied Energy*, *Marine and Petroleum
  Geology*, *Journal of Natural Gas Science and Engineering*.
- Springer: *Journal of Petroleum Exploration and Production Technology*
  (often open access), *Petroleum Science*.
- Wiley: *Geophysical Prospecting*, *Basin Research*.
- MDPI *Energies* — open access, high volume, uneven quality. Usable, but
  weight the impact score down and read the method section before citing.
- `arxiv.org` (physics.geo-ph, cs.LG) — for machine-learning-on-subsurface
  work that has not reached a journal. Treat as E3 at best unless also
  published; say it is a preprint.

## Regulators, agencies and national data

Filings and agency reports are underrated: they are open, quantitative, and
carry real operational detail.

- **US** — BSEE and BOEM (Gulf of Mexico approvals, production data),
  EIA (`eia.gov`), NETL and DOE (`netl.doe.gov` — field demonstration
  results, open, often E2), Texas RRC, New Mexico OCD, North Dakota DMR.
- **UK/Norway** — North Sea Transition Authority (`nstauthority.co.uk`),
  Norwegian Offshore Directorate (`sodir.no`) and its factpages.
- **Latin America** (high relevance to this firm) — ANP Brazil (`gov.br/anp`),
  Argentina Secretaría de Energía and IAPG, ANH Colombia, MEM Peru,
  PDVSA/Venezuela ministry disclosures where available.
- **Multilateral** — IEA, OPEC MOMR, IRENA for adjacent energy transition.

## Operator and vendor technical disclosure (E3)

Useful for field-trial numbers you cannot get anywhere else. Read them as
interested parties: cite the number, attribute it to the vendor, never repeat a
marketing claim as an independent finding.

- Service companies: SLB, Halliburton, Baker Hughes, Weatherford, TechnipFMC,
  Expro, Core Laboratories, NOV.
- Software and modelling: SLB (Petrel, Eclipse, Intersect), Aspen Tech
  (Petroleum Suite, HYSYS), Emerson/Paradigm, CMG, Halliburton Landmark
  (DecisionSpace), Ikon Science, S&P Global (Kingdom), dGB (OpendTect),
  Resoptima, Earth Science Analytics, Bluware, Katalyst.
- Operators' technical papers and conference presentations — Petrobras, Equinor,
  ADNOC, Aramco, Shell, ExxonMobil, Occidental, YPF, Ecopetrol.
- Open-source and consortium: OSDU (`osduforum.org`) — data platform standards
  are a genuine development; MRST, OPM Flow, Delft, SINTEF; Stanford SUPRI-B;
  university consortia (UT Austin CPGE, Texas A&M, Heriot-Watt, IFP).

## Trade press (E4 — corroboration and commercial news)

- Upstream Online, Hart Energy (*Oil and Gas Investor*, *E&P*), World Oil,
  Offshore Engineer, Offshore Magazine, Rigzone, JPT news pages,
  Oil & Gas Journal, Energy Voice, BNamericas (Latin America).
- Wire and price: Reuters, Argus, Platts, Bloomberg — for trading, sales,
  transport and differentials, where they are the *primary* source and count
  as E2 for market data even though they are E4 for technology claims.

## Discipline checklist

Sweep every one of these each month. Note explicitly in the dossier when a
discipline turned up nothing worth reporting — a quiet month in one area is
information, and silence must not look like an oversight.

| Discipline | Start with | Typical topics |
|---|---|---|
| Seismic acquisition & processing | SEG, EAGE | nodes, OBN, blended sources, FWI, LFE |
| Seismic interpretation | SEG *Interpretation*, AAPG | ML fault/horizon picking, attribute workflows, QI |
| Geological modelling | AAPG, GSL, SEPM | multipoint statistics, GANs for facies, structural restoration |
| Petrophysical evaluation | SPWLA, SPE REE | NMR, dielectric, log-based ML, core-log integration, thin beds |
| Reservoir engineering & simulation | SPE J, SPE RSC | compositional, EOR, CCUS storage, proxy/ROM models, ensemble methods |
| Production & artificial lift | SPE P&O | ESP/gas-lift optimisation, flow assurance, virtual metering, ICV/ICD |
| Drilling & completions | SPE D&C, IADC | geosteering, managed pressure, fracture diagnostics, refrac, plug-and-perf vs sliding sleeve |
| Well integrity & abandonment | SPE, AMPP, NSTA | barrier verification, P&A cost reduction, casing repair |
| Surface facilities | ASME, API, OGJ | compact separation, modular processing, electrification, produced-water handling |
| Transport & midstream | API, ASME, OGJ | pipeline integrity/ILI, LNG, flow assurance, hydrogen/CO2 blending |
| Trading, sales & commercial | Argus, Platts, Reuters, IEA | differentials, quality banks, contract structures, digital MRV |
| Digital & data | OSDU, SPE Digital Energy | physics-informed ML, agentic workflows, data standards, cloud HPC |
| Emissions & measurement | IOGP, EDF, DOE, EPA | methane detection, satellite MRV, flare reduction, CCUS monitoring |

## Search discipline

- Constrain by date. Every query should carry the target window
  (e.g. `2026`, `"July 2026"`, `last 60 days`). Undated results are the main
  route to stale or recycled findings.
- Search the discipline vocabulary, not just "oil and gas technology". Use the
  terms in the table above; a query like `full waveform inversion ocean bottom
  node 2026 SEG` returns something useful, `latest seismic technology` does not.
- Search in Spanish and Portuguese for Latin America items
  (`recuperación mejorada`, `modelagem de reservatório`). The regional societies
  publish in-language and the trade press translates late.
- Chase the citation. When trade press reports a result, find the underlying
  paper and cite that instead. If you cannot find it, the finding is E4.
- Check the ledger at `insights-data/radar-ledger.json` before writing. If a
  development already appeared in a previous edition, only revisit it when
  there is genuinely new evidence, and say what changed.
