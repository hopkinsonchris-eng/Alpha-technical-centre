# Wave 4 — Practice Scan: research runs per project and field

Brief from Chris, 1 October 2026, after first live use of wave 3: "If it does not find the fields in GeoNames then it should search for them. The system should automatically look for everything it can find about High Tech Electronica in Venezuela, everything about the fields; the system should spend the next hour populating its Vault." Wave 3's W3-D2 had left per-field research as follow-ups F13 (miners per field) and F14 (web search); this wave builds it.

Scope of the scan: how the best systems research an entity on demand and file what they find with provenance. Direct (competitive-intelligence and energy data platforms), adjacent (legal e-discovery, investigative journalism tooling, enterprise search agents). Rows P30–P37 continue the firm's numbering. Rows marked † were checked against the live source during this session (World Monitor's OpenAPI bundle; the Vault's own miner contract); the others are from the design pack's earlier research and general knowledge of the products, not re-verified today.

| # | Practice | Who does it | Why it is good | Cost to adopt |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P30 † | **Entity-scoped research against feeds the firm already pays for.** A company or country page runs a fixed set of queries against the subscribed services and shows each section with its freshness. | World Monitor (`search-gdelt-documents`, `get-company-enrichment`, `list-company-signals`, `search-sec-filings`, `get-intel-timeline`); Bloomberg company pages; Wood Mackenzie asset pages | Zero marginal cost; every result already carries a URL, a source and a date; the sections never pretend to be exhaustive. | Low: the adapter from wave 3 PR 4 plus one query builder. |
| P31 † | **Reuse the standing miners with a scoped topic.** The literature and regulator feeds that run weekly for the firm's topics also answer a one-off query for a field or operator. | The Vault's own M11 miners (`TopicSpec{query, keywords, negative}` → Semantic Scholar, Crossref, OpenAlex; ANH, ANP, Perupetro, Argentina; SEC EDGAR; EIA) | Already deduplicate on DOI and external id, screen off-topic records, and file immutable items with provenance. | Low: a `TopicSpec` per field and operator, a project scope on the run. |
| P32 | **Budgeted runs that stop and report.** A research job has a wall-clock and spend ceiling, files what it found, and says what it did not reach; a re-run continues from the cursor. | Recorded Future "collection" jobs; Elasticsearch enrich policies; Anthropic task budgets | Partners trust a run that says "15 minutes, 42 findings, stopped at GDELT page 3" over one that silently thins out. | Low: a `jobs` row with a summary and a per-source cursor. |
| P33 | **Findings are records, not chat.** Each finding is filed with source, URL, date, the query that found it and a verbatim excerpt, so the brief and the drafting assistant can cite it. | Palantir Foundry object provenance; Relativity (e-discovery) with Bates numbering; the Vault's own `[doc:…]` citations | Nothing the model "knows" enters the Vault without a record behind it (rule 4 of the firm's method). | Already the Vault's model: `items` with `origin` and `extracted`. |
| P34 | **Extraction proposes, people confirm.** A finding that names a new field, operator, licence or production figure opens a proposal; a person accepts it before it is a fact of the project. | Wave 3 asset proposals (`review_queue` kind `asset`); Wikidata's reference-required edits; Google Knowledge Graph curation | Keeps the hierarchy clean when a GDELT article names six fields in passing. | Low: reuse `extractAssets`; one new review kind for the rest. |
| P35 | **Trigger on the events that change the question.** Research runs when the entity changes (created, a field attached) and on demand, not on a timer. | GitHub Dependabot; Recorded Future watch lists; Google Alerts | Fresh when it matters, quiet otherwise; the standing weekly miners still cover the slow drift. | Low: two hooks and a button. |
| P36 | **Open-web search as a licensed, quoted tool, not a crawler.** Where a server-side search tool exists, every fetched page is cited by URL and quoted verbatim. | Anthropic `web_search_20260209` / `web_fetch_20260209`; Perplexity's citation model | The only honest way to say "the web says". | Medium: per-search pricing; provider changes. **Not chosen at Gate 1** (W4-D1). |
| P37 | **A research tab beside the timeline.** Findings are grouped by source and date with their quotes, searchable like everything else, and the run's status line sits at the top. | Notion AI research views; Bloomberg "News" tab per security | A partner sees what the Vault learned and when, without opening twenty records. | Low: one tab on the project file. |
