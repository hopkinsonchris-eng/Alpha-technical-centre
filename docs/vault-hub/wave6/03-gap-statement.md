# Wave 6 — Gap Statement

Nobody currently combines three things that each exist apart: per-person, consented capture of a whole mailbox (the relationship CRMs do this: Affinity, Attio), filing under a firm-level confidentiality scope where a message is one record whoever received it and is invisible to anyone outside its project (the legal document systems do this: iManage, NetDocuments), and a daily account of what moved on each opportunity that cites the records behind every line (nobody does this: CRMs show an activity feed, DMS show a filed tick, mail clients show an inbox). The evidence is the practice scan: Affinity and Attio file against people and companies, not matters, and have no scope below the workspace; iManage and NetDocuments file into matters but know nothing of consent or of who should call whom; Salesforce and HubSpot show unmatched mail in a holding feed and leave the reading to the person. The Vault already has the two halves that make the third possible: a classifier with evidence per message and an opportunity register with counterparties. Doing it would win the thing Chris asked for in one sentence: a Hub that loads everything, finds everything and never forgets, which a partner opens first each morning because it tells them, with proof, what changed overnight on the opportunities they hold.
