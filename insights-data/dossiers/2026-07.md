## 1. Added: MethaneSAT basin intensities (now the lead item)

Williams, Gautam et al., *Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics* 26, 5961-5981,
published 5 May 2026. Peer-reviewed, CC-BY open access, datasets public on

https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/26/5961/2026/

E1, readiness 3. Impact scoring: magnitude 5, breadth 5, adoption cost 5, time
to value 5, workflow relevance 4. **Mean 4.8**, which puts it above the

Why it was missed first time: the first pass searched the petroleum societies and
trade press, and this work sits in atmospheric science. The discipline checklist
had "emissions and measurement" on it, but the search never left the O&G
literature. **Fix for future editions: emissions, CCS monitoring and geothermal

It also repairs the regional gap on its own: six basins across the US, Mexico,
Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Iran and Iraq.

## 3. Explained: why the Middle East and Asia-Pacific were empty

Not purely a search failure. **Conference seasonality.** ADIPEC 2026 is 2-5
November; ADIPEC 2025 was November 2025. HFTC is February, OTC is May, ATCE is
autumn. A June-July window therefore falls between the major Middle East and
North American conference cycles, and journal output carries those months.

Implication for the series: regional coverage will oscillate with the conference
calendar unless journals and regulators are worked deliberately. The August
edition should not read a quiet ADIPEC month as a quiet region.

## 4. Seismic: searched twice, still nothing in window

Second attempt used different vocabulary (FWI, field results, method papers).
Returned 2023 to 2025 material plus one March 2026 arXiv preprint on a
physics-driven GAN for FWI, which is out of window and preprint tier. Recorded
as a genuine failure to reach SEG and EAGE 2026 output through general search.
The August edition must go at library.seg.org and earthdoc.org directly.

## Not added, and why

The ResFrac review listed nine further HFTC 2026 papers with quantified findings
(FR-coated proppant at 23-38% production increase; 0.1 uniformity-index increase
worth 7% production across 200 Bakken wells; HFTS-2 modelling showing ~40%
fracture height drainage). All readable, all quantified, all tempting.

**Excluded on window discipline.** They are February 2026 papers that surfaced in
February 2026, so unlike SPE 230613 they have no in-window surfacing to justify
them. Including them would have made this a completions-heavy retrospective of
HFTC rather than a current radar. They are held for a possible standalone
conference review piece.
