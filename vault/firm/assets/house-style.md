# ATC house style (letters and reports)

- Language: English or Spanish as the counterparty prefers; never mixed in one document.
- Reference line: `Our ref: ATC-<yyyy>-<nnnn>` and, when replying, `Your ref: <their reference>`.
- Date in words: 29 September 2026 / 29 de septiembre de 2026.
- Open with the purpose in the first sentence; one idea per paragraph; no bullet lists in legal letters.
- Every number cites its run or document in the working draft (`[run:…]`, `[doc:…]`); citations are stripped from the sent version and kept in the vault copy.
- Sign-off: "Yours sincerely" (named addressee) / "Yours faithfully" (unnamed); Spanish: "Atentamente".
- Typography follows the brand: Playfair Display for the wordmark, Barlow for body, navy #0B1F3A, gold #C9A84C.
- Prose must pass the humaniser rule: no machine cadence, no filler, every claim sourced.
